# Ethics and responsible use

SPDB documents ethical constraints for collecting, annotating, and redistributing
Spanish political text used in this resource.

## Scope of materials

SPDB targets **public political communication**, primarily parliamentary
interventions from ParlaMint-ES. It does **not** target private citizens'
communication or non-public personal data.

## Legal and redistribution status (v1.0.0)

| Slice | Redistribution in v1.0.0 | Status |
|-------|--------------------------|--------|
| Parliamentary text (ParlaMint-ES) | Yes, with attribution | Upstream **CC BY 4.0** |
| SPDB annotations / guidelines / schemas | Yes | **CC BY 4.0** |
| FallacyES-Political and similar external corpora | Not redistributed | Obtain upstream; use mapping scripts |
| Manifestos / social-media full text | Not included | N/A |

See [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) and [`docs/sources/parlamint.md`](sources/parlamint.md).

## Annotator welfare

Annotators code political content that may include attacks or inflammatory
rhetoric. Guidelines include escalation paths and adjudication notes for
borderline cases. Public Wave-1 exports use role labels (annotator A/B) rather
than personal contact data.

## Misuse considerations

SPDB is intended for research on political discourse structure, annotation
quality, and modelling—not for automated targeting of individuals, voter
manipulation, or surveillance.

Users must:

- Respect original source licences and platform terms
- Cite SPDB and underlying corpora when publishing derivatives
- Not present this diagnostic resource as adjudicated gold

## Human subjects review

Materials are public parliamentary speech. No additional identifiable private
data were collected for Wave-1. Formal IRB/ethics board approval was not
required for this archival public-speech annotation study under the authors'
institutional practice for this work.
